In practice

A day at the gym with the system

  1. 6:00 AMMembers arrive. Trainer types a name in Quick check-in. Expired members are flagged right there.
  2. 11:00 AMOpens the dashboard: three memberships end this week. Three taps send three WhatsApp reminders.
  3. 6:00 PMA member renews and pays ₹4,000 by UPI. Renew, enter the amount, save. Dues update instantly.
  4. 9:00 PMA website enquiry comes in. Reply on WhatsApp, book the trial, convert to member next day.

Members check their workout and diet in the app all day, so the trainer gets far fewer “what do I do today?” messages.
