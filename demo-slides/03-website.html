Part 1 · Website

Designed for the phone first

  • Most visitors come from Instagram on a phone, so the site is built for small screens first, with a simple menu and the free-trial button always in reach.
  • Quiet navy and white with serif headings. The gym’s own logo and photos are uploaded from the dashboard.
  • Shows if the gym is open right now (India time) and today’s hours. No prices on the website: visitors ask for a call back.
  • “Book a free trial” and WhatsApp are always one tap away.
  • Rendered on the server and marked up as a local business, so Google can list it.
  • Installable as an app (PWA), and pages still open on a weak connection.
