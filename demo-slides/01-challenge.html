The problem

Running a gym on registers and WhatsApp

  • Membership end dates live in a notebook, so renewals get missed and money is lost.
  • Dues are hard to track when people pay part cash, part UPI.
  • Every member asks for their workout and diet on WhatsApp, again and again.
  • New people can only find the gym by word of mouth: no website, no Google presence.
