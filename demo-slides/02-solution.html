The solution

Three parts online, or one offline app on the trainer’s PC

Online · For everyone

Portfolio website

Programs, plans, reviews and a free-trial form. Built for phones, found on Google.

Online · For the trainer

Gym dashboard

Members, renewals, offline payments, dues, attendance, plans, enquiries.

Online · For members

Member app (PWA)

Installs from the browser. Days left, dues, workout, diet, progress.

Alternative · Offline

Desktop app

Dashboard only, installed on the trainer’s Windows PC. Works with no internet, ₹0 running cost.

No member app or website leads. Data lives on one PC, so backups are a must.
