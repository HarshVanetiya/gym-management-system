The solution

Three parts, one login system, one database

For everyone

Portfolio website

Coach profile, programs, pricing, transformations and an enquiry form. Found on Google, and the trainer edits all of it.

For the trainer

Gym dashboard

Members, renewals, offline payments and dues, attendance, workout and diet plans, enquiries, reminders.

For members

Member app

Days left, pending dues, today’s workout, diet plan, progress and payment history, on their phone.
