Part 2 · Payments

Cash and UPI, recorded in seconds

  • No payment gateway and no fees: the trainer records what he receives.
  • Each payment is split by method: cash, UPI, card, bank.
  • The last 6 months at a glance. Tap a month to see every payment.
  • Members see the same history in their app, which avoids disputes.

An online gateway can be added later. See “Add-ons”.
