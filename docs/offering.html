Medilife Fitness Club — Features

What’s included

1. Website

  • Gym name, tagline, logo and photos
  • “Open now” status and opening hours
  • Training offered and member reviews
  • Photo gallery
  • Directions, call, WhatsApp and Instagram buttons
  • Free-trial form (goes to the dashboard as an enquiry)
  • Works on phones and computers, light and dark mode
  • Prices are not shown on the website

2. Trainer dashboard (admin)

  • Overview: active members, expiring, dues, collection, check-ins, enquiries
  • Member onboarding: add member, pick plan, record first payment
  • Enquiry → member in one click
  • Plans and renewals, with expiry lists
  • Payments: cash/UPI, dues, monthly report
  • WhatsApp reminders for renewals and dues
  • Workout and diet plans: create once, assign to members
  • Attendance check-in
  • Website editor: text, logo, photos, reviews
  • Backup and restore, CSV export

3. Member app

  • Membership: plan, days left, visits
  • Pending dues with UPI ID
  • Workout schedule for each day
  • Diet plan with meal times
  • Weight progress chart
  • Payment history; profile photo and phone number

4. Install as an app (no Play Store)

  • Installs on Android, iPhone and computers directly from the website. No Play Store or App Store needed.
  • An Install app button is always available. If the phone doesn’t show the install pop-up, the button shows the steps for that phone.
  • Opens full screen like a normal app and updates automatically.

5. Access

  • Visitors: website and free-trial form
  • Members: only their own plan, diet, progress and payments
  • Trainer: full access to everything
  • Secure login for members and trainer

6. Running cost

  • Hosting, database and photo storage on free plans.
  • Only the domain name: about ₹900–1,500 a year.
