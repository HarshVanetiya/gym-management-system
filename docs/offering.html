02 · Portfolio website

A calm, classy website that brings in members

What visitors see

  • The gym’s name, tagline and a short introduction, with the gym’s own logo and photos.
  • Live “Open now · until 10 PM” status in India time, and the weekly hours with today highlighted.
  • Training offered: strength, weight loss, personal training and diet plans.
  • Member reviews and an “Inside the gym” photo gallery.
  • Address, one-tap directions, call, WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • No prices on the website. Visitors book a free trial or ask for a call back.

How it works for the gym

  • Free-trial form: name, mobile, goal and preferred time. It lands in the dashboard as an enquiry.
  • Built for phones first, with a simple menu and the “Free trial” button always in reach.
  • Light and dark mode.
  • Shown to Google as a local business (name, hours, phone, address).
  • The trainer edits all text, the logo, the main photo and the gallery from the dashboard. No developer needed.
  • Fast on weak mobile data. Photos are resized automatically.

03 · Trainer dashboard

Run the whole gym from one screen

Daily overview

Active members, memberships expiring this week, pending dues, money collected this month, today’s check-ins and new enquiries.

Member onboarding

Add a member with contact details, goal and notes, pick a plan and record the first payment in one step. Turn a website enquiry into a member with one click.

Member app access

Save the member’s email and they sign up with it. Their plan, diet and payments appear automatically. The dashboard shows who has linked their login.

Plans and renewals

Set up plans (monthly, quarterly, personal training…). Renew in a few taps, with expiry and “expired” lists so no renewal is missed.

Offline payments

Record cash or UPI payments with a reference note. Dues are tracked per member, with a monthly report and CSV export.

WhatsApp reminders

One tap opens WhatsApp with a ready-written renewal or dues reminder, including the gym’s UPI ID.

Workouts and diets

Weekly workout templates (exercises, sets, reps, notes) and diet templates (meals, times, calories). Assign them to members, and change a template once to update everyone on it.

Attendance

Quick check-in by name or phone number, and each member’s visit history.

Enquiries, website and backup

Enquiries list with status. Website editor for text, logo and photos. Members CSV export, full backup and restore, and storage usage.

04 · Member app

Every member’s plan, in their pocket

Members log in with their own email and see only their own information.

  • Membership card: plan, start and end date, days left, and visits in the last 30 days.
  • Dues: pending amount with the gym’s UPI ID, or pay cash at the desk.
  • Workout schedule: the exercises, sets and reps for each day of the week.
  • Diet plan: meals with times and portions, set by the coach.
  • Progress: log body weight and see the trend on a chart.
  • Payments history, and a profile where members add their photo and mobile number.

05 · App without Play Store

Installs like an app, straight from the website

The system is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Members and the trainer install it from the browser on Android, iPhone or a computer. There is no Play Store or App Store listing, no store fees and no waiting for approval.

What members get

  • An MFC icon on the home screen that opens full screen, like any app.
  • Opens on weak connections. Pages already visited load from the phone.
  • Always up to date. New features arrive without re-installing.
  • Takes almost no storage compared with a store app.
  • The installed app opens straight to “My plan”. The trainer’s opens to the dashboard.

An “Install app” button for every phone

Some phones show an automatic install pop-up and many don’t: iPhones, Samsung Internet, Firefox, the Instagram browser, or after someone closes the pop-up once. So the website, the member app and the dashboard always show an Install app button. It opens the install pop-up where the phone supports it, and otherwise shows the exact steps for that phone.

06 · Access, data and running costs

Data and safety

  • Secure email and password login. Access rules are enforced by the database itself, so one member can never open another member’s data.
  • One-click backup file of all gym data, and restore into a new account if the gym moves.
  • A clean-up tool removes old check-ins and closed enquiries to keep storage small.
  • Measured: about 11 MB of data a year for 300 members, against 500 MB on the free plan.

Running costs

  • Database and logins (Supabase), hosting (Netlify) and photos (Cloudinary): free plans.
  • Domain name: about ₹900–1,500 a year (.in ≈ ₹899, .com ≈ ₹1,499 at renewal).
  • No payment-gateway fees: payments are cash or UPI at the desk.
  • Optional later: online payments, automatic WhatsApp messages, QR check-in, and an offline desktop version of the dashboard.

What we need from the gym to go live

Logo, 6–10 photos (gym floor, equipment, trainer), opening hours, phone and WhatsApp number, UPI ID, membership plans, and a few real member reviews.
